1. Information stored

Your chats, projects, API keys and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. You sign in with a RanEddie ID.

RanEddie ID

If you sign in with RanEddie ID, your account (email, username, date of birth and country you gave at sign-up) is managed by RANEDDIE through Clerk, our authentication provider. FIZZ saves only a few small settings to your account — theme, response style, AI model and FIZZ DEV mode — so they follow you to other devices. Your chats, projects and API keys are never sent to your account.

2. AI requests

When you send a message, its content, attachments and the results of the tools the AI uses are sent to the AI provider you chose in Settings → AI: FIZZ AI (through FIZZ's server), OpenRouter, Google AI Studio or Anthropic (directly from your browser with your own key).

Google user data

FIZZ DEV can connect to your Google account only when you choose to, through Google's own sign-in window. With your permission it can use Gmail (read, search, write drafts and send emails), Google Calendar (read, create and delete events), Google Docs, Sheets and Slides (create, read and edit), Google Drive (search, read and upload files), Google Tasks and Google Contacts (read). It does this only to carry out what you ask Fizz Code to do in a chat. Sending an email, inviting people to an event and deleting an event always wait for your confirmation.

How Google data is handled

Your Google access token stays in your browser tab and expires after about an hour; it is never sent to or stored on FIZZ's servers. Requests go directly from your browser to Google. The Google data needed for a task (for example the emails Fizz Code found) is shown to the AI provider you chose so it can complete the task, and is kept in that chat in your browser (and in your RanEddie ID cloud sync if you turned it on). FIZZ does not sell Google data, does not use it for advertising, does not use it to train AI models, and does not let anyone read it except to provide the feature you asked for, for security, or to comply with the law. You can disconnect Google at any time in Settings → Connections, and remove FIZZ's access in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting a chat deletes the Google data in it.

Limited Use

FIZZ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Support messages

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to RANEDDIE's support team.

4. Your rights

You can export your data or delete your account at any time from Settings.

5. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: use the contact page (contact.html) or email raneddie321@gmail.com.
