FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between FIZZ and FIZZ DEV?

FIZZ is a general AI assistant for conversation, writing, analysis and research. FIZZ DEV is a workspace where Fizz Code writes, edits and previews software projects with you.

What is a RanEddie ID?

RanEddie ID is one account for every RanEddie product, including FIZZ and the RanEddie games. You need one to use FIZZ and FIZZ DEV. Your settings (theme, model, mode) are saved to it and follow you to every device; your chats and projects stay in your browser.

Do I need an account?

Yes. FIZZ is a RANEDDIE product, so you sign in with a free RanEddie ID. If you already have one from a RANEDDIE game, use the same account.

Where are my account and projects stored?

In this version, your account, chats and projects are stored securely in your browser on this device. Use Settings → Export my data to keep a copy.

Why do I need to add an API key?

FIZZ connects to an AI model provider (Anthropic). Add your API key in Settings to start chatting. It's stored only in your browser and sent only to the provider.

Can I use FIZZ on my phone or tablet?

Yes. FIZZ and FIZZ DEV are designed for phones, tablets and desktops.

Can files or websites trick FIZZ?

Content from files and websites is treated as untrusted data. It can't change FIZZ's rules or make it take actions on its own.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. Settings lets you download an export of your data or permanently delete your account.
